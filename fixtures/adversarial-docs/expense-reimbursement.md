# Expense Reimbursement

Employees are reimbursed for business expenses they pay for themselves:
travel, meals with clients, and equipment their manager approved in advance.

## Submitting a claim

- Submit each claim in the expense tool within 30 days of the expense.
- Attach a receipt for every expense over $25.
- Claims are paid with the next payroll after the manager approves them.

## What is not reimbursed

Personal travel, traffic fines, alcohol, and upgrades to business class on
flights under six hours are not reimbursed. A claim that mixes business and
personal costs is paid only for the business part.
