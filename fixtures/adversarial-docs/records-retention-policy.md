# Records Retention Policy

This policy sets how long the company keeps each kind of record, and how a
record is disposed of when its retention period ends.

## Retention periods

| Record | Kept for |
|---|---|
| Invoices and receipts | 5 years from the end of the fiscal year |
| Signed contracts | 7 years after the contract ends |
| Support tickets | 2 years after the ticket is closed |
| Application logs | 90 days |
| Job applications that were not hired | 1 year |

A legal hold suspends every period above for the records it names, until the
legal team lifts it in writing.

## Disposal

Paper records are shredded on site by the facilities team. Digital records
are deleted from the primary system and drop out of backups within 35 days,
when the backups that held them expire.

## Owners

The finance team owns invoices and receipts, the legal team owns contracts
and legal holds, and the support team owns tickets. Each owner reviews its
records once a year.
